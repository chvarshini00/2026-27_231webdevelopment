Rules & Regulations

Please follow these guidelines during the fest.

  • Students must register before participating.
  • Participants should carry their college ID cards.
  • Follow the event schedule and reporting times.
  • Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  • Follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Submit projects within the announced deadlines.
  • The judges' decisions will be final.
  • Keep the campus clean and protect college property.
